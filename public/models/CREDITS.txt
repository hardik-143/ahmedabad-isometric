Walk-mode characters and animations by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain).
- man.glb / woman.glb: Universal Base Characters [Standard], base-colour textures only, resized to JPEG.
- anims.glb: Universal Animation Library [Standard], the 14 clips walk mode uses, mesh removed.
Clothes, hair and colours are added in the app (public/app.js).
