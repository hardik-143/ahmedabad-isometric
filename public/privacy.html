Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Short version: you can play without an account. If you sign in, we keep your email, your name and your avatar so your character follows you to any device. We don't sell your data and there are no ads.

Who we are

GTA Ahmedabad (gta-ahmedabad.thehardik.in) is a free 3D game of Ahmedabad that runs in your browser. It is made by Hardik Desai. Contact: dhardik1430@gmail.com.

Playing as a guest

Without an account, your avatar choices (name, clothes, colours, the vehicle colour you picked) and where you last were in the city are saved only in your own browser's local storage on that device. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Signing in

Sign-in is optional and is handled by Clerk, our account provider. When you create an account:

  • With email: Clerk stores your email address and sends you a one-time code to confirm it.
  • With Google: Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We ask for nothing else: no access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • What the game saves to your account: your avatar settings and your last position in the city, so they load on any device you sign in on.

We use this information only to run your account and your game profile. We don't send you marketing emails.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which devices and countries people play from, so we know what to improve. It uses cookies and collects things like the pages you open, your device and browser type, and an approximate location. It does not receive your account details.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and the time of each request) for security and reliability.

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we don't show ads. Your data is shared only with the services above (Clerk, Google Analytics, Vercel), which process it for us, or where the law requires it.

Deleting your data

  • Guest: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Account: in the game, tap Account (top bar) → Security → Delete account, or email dhardik1430@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and everything saved with it.

Children

The game is for a general audience. Accounts are not meant for children under 13; if you believe a child has created one, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
